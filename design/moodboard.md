# Fizz — how this site is built

This is the **built** form of the template *Fizz*: lime green on sunlit
photographs, a handwritten word over every heading, everything rounded. It was
built to the owner's picture (`design/template.jpg`), and a project that picks
the template starts from it.

There is no direction to choose here. What is left to decide is how one
business sits in it:

- the **first screen is one bright photograph** of the thing being enjoyed,
  with ONE WORD set as wide as the page across its foot. It has to look like a
  hot afternoon: an indoor, grey or moody picture is the wrong picture for
  this look, whatever it shows;
- **three faces, three jobs**: the heavy condensed capitals for headings, a
  plain sans for reading, and the script for three or four handwritten words
  across the END of a heading (`<Script>`) — a mark, never a sentence;
- **one lime, with dark type on it**, for every chip and button. It never
  goes onto a photograph, only onto what is laid over one;
- a thing for sale **stands cut out on sky**, with its one fact on a lime chip;
- the notes band is the shop's own words. This system has nobody's reviews;
  with real ones, each card takes the reviewer's name;
- the hues may be re-tinted to the business — one loud accent, one deep ink.

What the template has that this system cannot make: designed packaging shot
in a studio and in water, and a subject cut out so the big word passes behind
them. Here the word lies over the photograph, pale enough to read through.

## COMMITMENTS

- A thin strip in the lime accent runs across the very top of the page carrying one line in small capitals.
- The first screen is one bright photograph with one word set in heavy capitals as wide as the page across its foot, white and half-transparent, and a few handwritten words in lime over it.
- The header is one rounded bar of glass lying over the top of the photograph: the name in heavy capitals, the links, the cart with its count and a white pill.
- Two small white rounded cards float over the photograph's lower corners, each carrying a thing for sale, its name, its price and a round lime button.
- Every heading is heavy condensed capitals in deep green with a few handwritten lime words tilted across its end.
- The range is a row of rounded cards, each with the thing standing on a sky-blue tile, a small lime chip at its top right, and its name, price and a round lime button beneath.
- The footer ends on the name set in the heavy capitals as wide as the page and cut off by its bottom edge.
